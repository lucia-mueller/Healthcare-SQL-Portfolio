# Healthcare SQL Portfolio

This portfolio showcases SQL projects focused on healthcare data analysis, operational reporting, risk stratification, and performance measurement. The projects use fictional datasets to demonstrate practical SQL skills and analytical approaches applicable to healthcare operations and value-based care.

## Projects

### [Provider Productivity Dashboard](https://github.com/lucia-mueller/Healthcare-SQL-Portfolio/blob/main/Provider-Productivity-Dashboard)

Analyzes fictional provider and patient visit data to evaluate provider productivity, appointment volume, and visit completion rates.

**Key analyses include:**

- Total visits by provider
- Average visits per provider
- Identification of providers with no recorded visits
- Monthly appointment volume trends
- Provider-level visit completion rates

**SQL techniques demonstrated:**

- LEFT JOIN
- GROUP BY
- Aggregate functions
- CASE statements
- Date functions
- NULL handling

➡️ [View the full Provider Productivity Dashboard project](https://github.com/lucia-mueller/Healthcare-SQL-Portfolio/blob/main/Provider-Productivity-Dashboard)

### [Healthcare Claims & Risk Analysis](https://github.com/lucia-mueller/Healthcare-SQL-Portfolio/blob/main/Healthcare-Claims-Risk-Analysis)

Analyzes a fictional healthcare claims dataset to evaluate patient clinical risk, healthcare utilization, claim costs, and provider patient panels.

**Key analyses include:**

- Patient-level diagnosis and HCC risk burden
- Identification of high-risk and high-cost patients
- Total claim cost by assigned provider panel
- Emergency department and inpatient utilization analysis
- Monthly claim cost trends
- Patient risk versus healthcare cost segmentation

**SQL techniques demonstrated:**

- Multi-table JOINs
- GROUP BY and aggregate functions
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- CASE statements and conditional aggregation
- Window functions
- LAG()
- DENSE_RANK()
- Date functions
