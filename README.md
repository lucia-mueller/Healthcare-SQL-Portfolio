# Healthcare SQL Portfolio

This portfolio showcases SQL projects focused on healthcare data analysis, operational reporting, risk stratification, quality performance, and value-based care. Each project uses fictional datasets to demonstrate practical SQL skills and analytical approaches applicable to healthcare operations, population health, and performance improvement.

---

## Featured Project

### ⭐ [Value-Based Care Quality Analysis](https://github.com/lucia-mueller/Healthcare-SQL-Portfolio/tree/main/Value-Based-Care-Quality-Analysis)

Healthcare quality analytics project examining care-gap completion, patient risk, provider performance, and clinic-level quality improvement opportunities.

**Highlights:**
- Identifies high-risk patients with unresolved quality gaps
- Compares quality performance across providers and clinics
- Prioritizes clinic-specific quality improvement opportunities
- Uses CTEs, conditional aggregation, multi-table JOINs, and DENSE_RANK(). 
- Translates SQL results into actionable value-based care insights

**Key analyses include:**

- Identification of providers with no recorded visits

### 3. [Value-Based Care Quality Analysis](https://github.com/lucia-mueller/Healthcare-SQL-Portfolio/tree/main/Value-Based-Care-Quality-Analysis)

Analyzes a fictional healthcare quality dataset to evaluate care-gap completion, provider and clinic performance, patient risk, and opportunities for targeted quality improvement.

**Key analyses include:**

- Overall quality measure completion rates
- Provider-level quality performance
- Quality performance by patient risk level
- Identification of high-risk patients with open care gaps
- Open care gaps by quality measure
- Clinic-level quality performance
- Clinic-specific care-gap opportunities
- Ranked quality improvement opportunities by clinic

The analysis demonstrates how healthcare data can be used to identify patients, providers, clinics, and quality measures with the greatest opportunities for intervention and care-gap closure.

**SQL techniques demonstrated:**

- Multi-table JOINs
- GROUP BY and aggregate functions
- COUNT(DISTINCT)
- CASE statements
- Conditional aggregation
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window functions
- DENSE_RANK()
- Patient-, provider-, and clinic-level segmentation
- Calculated completion and gap rates

## About This Portfolio

These projects were developed to demonstrate hands-on SQL and analytical skills relevant to healthcare data analytics, clinical operations, value-based care, risk adjustment, quality improvement, and performance measurement.

All patient, provider, diagnosis, visit, claims, clinic, and quality measure data used throughout this portfolio are entirely fictional and were created solely for portfolio and educational purposes. No real patient information or protected health information (PHI) is used.
